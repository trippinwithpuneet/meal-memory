Frequently asked questions

What is Meal Memory?

It's a shared weekly meal planner for your household. You plan breakfast, lunch, dinner and dessert on one grid that syncs across everyone's phone (free for two people, up to 6 on Pro), build recipes by importing links, get ideas from what's in your fridge, and shop from a grocery list that builds itself.

Is it free?

Yes. The free tier is a complete planner for one or two people, with no trial period and no card required.

Free includes: a shared weekly plan for up to 2 people that syncs live, week and month views, breakfast/lunch/dinner/dessert, up to 25 saved recipes, 10 recipe imports, a grocery list for the week, unlimited “What can I cook?” fridge suggestions, sharing your week as an image or text, and meal reminders.

The 25 recipes and 10 imports are one-off totals, not monthly allowances — once you've used them, adding more needs Pro. Everything else in the free tier is unlimited and stays free.

Meal Memory Pro ($2.99/month, $14.99/year, or $29.99 once for lifetime) adds unlimited recipe imports, a smart grocery list sorted by aisle that the household can check off and export, an unlimited recipe library, and a household of up to 6 with per-person dietary needs.

How do I share a plan with my partner or housemate?

Create your household and send an invite. Once they join, everyone sees the same weekly plan in real time and can add or change meals as equals — no single “owner.” Two people is free; Pro raises the household to 6.

Which recipe sources can I import from?

You can paste a link from TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Pinterest, or most recipe websites. Meal Memory pulls out the ingredients and steps so you get a clean, usable recipe instead of a screenshot.

An import didn't work — what do I do?

Some pages block automated reading or don't contain a structured recipe. Try the original source link (not a shortened or re-shared one). If it still fails, email us the link at support@mealmemory.app and we'll take a look — real import failures help us improve.

How does “What can I cook?” work?

Tell Meal Memory what you have in the fridge or pantry and it suggests recipes you can make right now. It's free and unlimited.

Is my data private?

Yes. We use no third-party advertising or tracking, and we never sell your data. Your plans and recipes are stored securely to sync across your household. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my account?

Email support@mealmemory.app from the address on your account and we'll delete your account and associated personal data.

How do I cancel or manage a subscription?

Meal Memory Pro subscriptions are billed through your Apple ID. Manage or cancel any time in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone. Lifetime purchases are one-time and don't renew.

I have a feature idea.

We'd love to hear it. Meal Memory is built by one developer who actually cooks, and user ideas shape the roadmap. Send it to support@mealmemory.app.

Still stuck?

Email us at support@mealmemory.app and we'll get you sorted.
