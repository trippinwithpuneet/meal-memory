Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 July 2026

Meal Memory is built by a solo indie developer who cooks. We collect the least data we can, we don't run ads, and we never sell your information. This policy explains, in plain language, what we store and why.

The short version: We store the meal-planning data you create (recipes, plans, grocery lists, household membership) so the app can sync it across your household. We use no third-party advertising or analytics trackers. We never sell your data.

1. Who we are

“Meal Memory,” “we,” “us,” and “our” refer to the developer of the Meal Memory mobile application and this website. You can reach us any time at support@mealmemory.app.

2. Information we collect

Information you provide

  • Account details. An email address (and, if you choose, a display name) used to create your account and connect you to your household.
  • Content you create. Meal plans, recipes you add or import, grocery lists, and notes. Imported recipes may include a source link and the ingredients and steps extracted from that link.
  • Household data. The household you create or join, and which members belong to it, so the plan can be shared and synced.
  • Support messages. If you email us, we keep that correspondence to help you.

Information collected automatically

  • Basic operational data. Our backend provider records standard technical information (such as request timestamps and error logs) needed to run and secure the service.
  • No third-party advertising or tracking SDKs. Meal Memory does not embed advertising networks or cross-app tracking. We do not build advertising profiles and we do not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA).

3. How we use your information

  • To provide the core service: saving your plans, syncing them across the members of your household, importing recipes, and generating grocery lists.
  • To authenticate you and keep your account and household secure.
  • To respond to support requests and, if you opt in, to send occasional product updates.
  • To diagnose problems and improve reliability using aggregated, non-identifying operational data.

We do not use your personal content to serve advertising, and we do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

4. Where your data is stored

Meal Memory uses Supabase (a hosted Postgres platform) as its backend to store and sync your data. Your data is held on Supabase's cloud infrastructure and protected in transit with encryption (HTTPS/TLS). Supabase acts as our data processor; you can review their practices at supabase.com/privacy.

5. Recipe imports and third-party links

When you import a recipe from a service such as TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Pinterest, or any website, Meal Memory retrieves the content at the link you provide in order to extract the recipe. We store the resulting recipe in your account. We are not affiliated with those platforms, and their handling of your activity on their own services is governed by their own privacy policies.

6. Sharing your information

We share your information only in these limited situations:

  • With your household. Content in a shared plan is visible to the members of that household, by design.
  • With service providers. Infrastructure providers (such as Supabase) that process data on our behalf so the app can function.
  • When shared by you. If you share your weekly plan as an image or text, that content goes wherever you choose to send it.
  • For legal reasons. If required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users and the service.

7. Data retention and deletion

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete individual content in the app at any time. To delete your account and associated personal data, email support@mealmemory.app and we will process the request. Some records may be retained where required for legal or security reasons.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to object to certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at support@mealmemory.app. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

9. Children's privacy

Meal Memory is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will remove it.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. When we make material changes, we will update the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

Questions about privacy? A real person reads every message.
