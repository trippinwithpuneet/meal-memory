Why home cooks love it

Everything you need to eat well, minus the daily scramble.

🗓️

One plan, one household

A shared weekly grid for breakfast, lunch, dinner and dessert that updates on everyone's phone the moment it changes. No more “did you defrost the chicken?” texts — the plan already knows. Free for two people; Pro takes it to six.

🤔

No more 6pm panic

Decide once on Sunday, coast all week. The nightly “what should we eat?” negotiation simply disappears.

🧊

Cook what's already there

Tell it what's in your fridge and get real recipes you can make tonight. Free, unlimited, forever.

📥

One-tap recipe imports

Saw it on TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, Pinterest or the web? Paste the link — it lands in your plan, clean and ready. Ten free imports to try it; unlimited on Pro.

🛒

A grocery list that writes itself

Your week's meals roll up into one list, free. Pro sorts it by aisle, lets the household tick items off together, and exports it wherever you shop.

Three steps to a calmer kitchen

From blank week to done.

1

Fill your week

Drag in favorites, import a link, or ask what you can cook. Build a full week in minutes.

2

Share the plan

Invite your partner or housemate — free for two. Everyone sees the same plan, live, on their own phone.

3

Shop & cook

Your week's ingredients are already gathered into a list. Shop once, and cook without the nightly guesswork.
