Grandma's Burrito Bowl

No plugin, no schema markup. This is the ~20% case that falls through to the LLM tail.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cooked rice
  • 1 can black beans, drained
  • 1 avocado, sliced
  • 1/2 cup corn
  • 1/4 cup salsa
  • Handful of cilantro

Method

  1. Warm the rice and beans.
  2. Layer rice, then beans, then corn in a bowl.
  3. Top with avocado, salsa and cilantro.
